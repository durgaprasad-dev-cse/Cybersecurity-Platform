🛡️ What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, servers, mobile devices, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or theft. It helps maintain the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

Essential Security Principles

Good cybersecurity begins with a few simple habits that can significantly reduce everyday digital risks.

🔐

Protect Your Accounts

Use strong, unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication whenever possible.

🎣

Think Before You Click

Be careful with unexpected links, attachments, messages and requests for sensitive information.

🔄

Keep Systems Updated

Keep your operating system, applications and security software updated.

Why It Matters

Why is Cybersecurity Important?

Cybersecurity helps individuals and organizations protect valuable information and maintain trust in digital systems.

🌐

Key Benefits

  • Protects personal and financial information
  • Helps prevent identity theft and fraud
  • Secures business and government systems
  • Maintains trust in digital platforms
  • Helps reduce data loss and service downtime
Protect Yourself

Data & Account Protection

Follow practical security habits when managing your information, devices and online accounts.

💾

How to Protect Your Data

  • Keep software and operating systems updated
  • Use antivirus and firewall protection
  • Backup important data regularly
  • Encrypt sensitive files and drives
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive transactions
🔑

Password Protection

  • Use strong passwords with multiple character types
  • Prefer longer passwords or passphrases
  • Never reuse the same password across important accounts
  • Consider using a reputable password manager
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
Stay Alert

Phishing & Social Engineering

Attackers may use deception and urgency to convince people to reveal information or perform unsafe actions.

🎣

Recognize Phishing Attempts

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers try to trick users into revealing sensitive information by pretending to be trusted entities through emails, messages or fake websites.

  • Verify sender email addresses and identities
  • Check website URLs carefully before entering information
  • Be cautious with urgent or threatening messages
  • Never share credentials through unexpected email or SMS requests
⚠️ Remember: Don't allow urgency or fear to make the security decision for you. Stop, verify and then act.
Avoid These

Common Bad Cyber Practices

Small careless actions can create opportunities for attackers.

🚨

Do NOT Do This

  • Click unknown or suspicious links
  • Share OTPs, passwords or authentication codes
  • Use extremely weak passwords such as 123456
  • Install cracked or pirated software
  • Ignore important security warnings
Understand The Impact

Potential Consequences of Cyber Attacks

Cyber incidents can affect individuals, organizations and their digital assets in different ways.

⚠️

Possible Losses

  • Financial loss and banking fraud
  • Loss or misuse of personal identity
  • Business reputation damage
  • Data leakage and privacy violations
  • Legal consequences and penalties
Quick Checklist

Cybersecurity Do's & Don'ts

Keep these simple rules in mind during everyday digital activities.

✔ Do's

  • Use legitimate and trusted software
  • Lock devices when they are not in use
  • Report suspicious activities
  • Continue learning about cybersecurity

❌ Don'ts

  • Do not blindly trust unknown sources
  • Do not share personal information publicly
  • Do not ignore important security updates
  • Do not enter sensitive information on suspicious websites
Take Action

What Should You Do If Something Looks Suspicious?

Don't panic. Follow a simple sequence before taking further action.

01

Stop

Don't immediately click, download or respond.

02

Check

Examine the sender, link, file or request carefully.

03

Verify

Confirm the request using a trusted source.

04

Report

Report suspicious activity through the appropriate channel.
